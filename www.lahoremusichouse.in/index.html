Why Lahore Music House

The Hallmark of True Acoustic Mastery

Every instrument crafted in our workshop represents four generations of acoustic knowledge and artisan dedication.

Seasoned Tonewoods

We source aged Sheesham, Burma Teak, and Red Tun wood seasoned over years to prevent warping and ensure pure acoustic resonance.

Master Ear Tuning

Every reed, string, and leather diaphragm is tuned by experienced ustads to accurate 440Hz concert pitch or custom microtonal scales.

Shockproof Export Packing

Multi-layered thermocol cushioning, moisture-barrier wrapping, and heavy-duty wooden crates protect shipments to anywhere on earth.

Custom Bespoke Orders

Specialized scale tuning, left-handed configurations, custom engraving, and bespoke bridge setups tailored for performing soloists.

State-of-the-Art Workshop

Where Traditional Craft Meets Stringent Quality Control

Our dedicated production workshop houses specialized carving stations, wood-drying seasoning facilities, and skin-curing rooms. Each artisan brings decades of familial woodcraft tradition, ensuring that every instrument resonates with emotional depth.
