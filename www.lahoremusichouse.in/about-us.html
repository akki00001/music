Lahore Music House is located in the Indian state of Delhi and running magnificently under the guidance of Mr. Jaspal Singh Sachdeva, one of the partners of the company. As a promising name, we started our business operations in 1947 with an aim of serving the clients with the best at economical prices.

Our Team of Professionals
We are a team of young people and dedicatedly work to attain the maximum satisfaction of the clients by delivering high-quality product line. As a knowledgeable team, we constantly improve ourselves and work together to ensure that the customers get the best. Our team of professionals includes :

  • Storehouse in-charge
  • Packaging experts
  • Logisticians
  • Marketing heads

Contribution Towards Quality
For us, quality is the major focus of our organization through which we can achieve complete client contentment. We have also adopted a system comprising strict parameters and inspection procedures that help us to deliver the best.
